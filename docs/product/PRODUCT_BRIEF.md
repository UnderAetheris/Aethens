# Product brief

## One line

**Aetheris is the personal AI that actually gets better at helping you, and proves it.**

## The problem

Every AI assistant today starts each day at the same level it was yesterday. It forgets what failed, repeats mistakes, can't show you whether it improved, and asks you to trust it with your files and PC without showing what it did. The ones that do "self-improve" rewrite their own prompts and memory with no measurement and no undo.

## The promise

1. **It learns from you and from its mistakes**, and keeps only what makes it measurably better.
2. **You can see the proof**: a level and scorecard measured on frozen benchmarks, a report of what it learned, and an undo for every change.
3. **It is safe to give real access**: one gate for every action, one gate for the network, approvals for anything risky, hard locks it can never cross.
4. **It looks after your machine**: health, security visibility, cleanup on approval.
5. **It costs nothing to run** beyond the model you already use, on an ordinary laptop.

## Who it is for

| Persona | Job to be done | Hook |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Solo builder / student dev** (primary, the owner) | Ship code faster, learn while doing, on a cheap laptop | Free, runs on 8 GB, gets better at *your* codebase |
| **Power user** | Delegate research, file chores, PC upkeep | Approvals inbox + Guardian + reports |
| **AI tinkerer** | Experiment with self-improving agents safely | Measured gates, rollback receipts, open architecture |

## Pillars (what we never compromise)

1. **Trust**: visible, reversible, explainable. No silent actions.
2. **Proof**: every "better" has a number.
3. **Calm**: it saves time, never adds noise. Batched updates, one-tap decisions.
4. **Craft**: it must feel built by a top product team, not vibecoded.

## Core loops users feel

- **Daily**: open Home -> see "N things need you" -> approve/deny in seconds -> read a 30-second overnight summary.
- **Working**: ask in Chat or Ctrl+K -> watch live plan and safety decisions -> get result + "why" on demand.
- **Weekly**: Level/scorecard goes up (or it honestly says why not) -> review proposed skills.

## Retention hooks (honest ones)

- Level and weekly scorecard grounded in real benchmarks
- "While you were away" AFK report
- Skills library that grows from your own work
- PC health streaks

| --- | --- |
